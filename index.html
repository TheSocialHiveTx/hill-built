Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about seamless gutter installation and maintenance.

Sectional gutters are pieced together in 10-foot lengths, creating seams every 10 feet that inevitably leak, collect debris, and sag over time. Seamless gutters are custom-extruded in continuous runs on-site, eliminating straight-run seams entirely.
While 5-inch gutters were traditional, we strongly recommend 6-inch oversized K-style gutters for Houston homes. A 6-inch gutter holds 40% more water volume, ensuring your roofline doesn't overflow during torrential Gulf Coast rainstorms.
Yes! Our surgical-grade stainless steel micro-mesh has micro-openings that allow only water to pass through, shedding pine needles, oak tassels, leaves, and shingle granules away safely.
